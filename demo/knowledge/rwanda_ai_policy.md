# Rwanda National Artificial Intelligence Policy

Source: Rwanda Ministry of ICT and Innovation / Rwanda Information Society Authority
Document: National Artificial Intelligence Policy

## Vision

Rwanda's National AI Policy sets the vision of becoming a global center for AI research and innovation.

## Mission

The policy seeks to leverage AI to power economic growth, improve quality of life, and position Rwanda as a global innovator for responsible and inclusive AI.

## National AI Objectives

The policy identifies objectives including:

- Positioning Rwanda as Africa's AI Lab and Responsible AI Champion.
- Building 21st Century Skills and AI Literacy.
- Creating an open, secure and trusted data ecosystem.
- Driving public-sector transformation through AI adoption.
- Accelerating responsible AI adoption in the private sector.

## Infrastructure and Computing

Reliable infrastructure and computing capacity are important enablers of Rwanda's AI ecosystem.

The policy recognizes the importance of access to scalable storage and high-performance computing resources.

## Responsible AI

Rwanda's AI strategy emphasizes responsible, inclusive and sustainable AI development.

## Relevance to QAIR

QAIR is a local AI runtime that demonstrates one practical implementation of local AI infrastructure:

- AI inference can run on local hardware.
- GGUF language models can operate without requiring an external AI API.
- Knowledge can be stored and retrieved locally.
- AI applications can therefore be designed around locally controlled inference infrastructure.

QAIR does not represent the Rwanda National AI Policy itself. It is a technical demonstration aligned with the policy's broader emphasis on AI capability, infrastructure, responsible innovation and local ecosystem development.
