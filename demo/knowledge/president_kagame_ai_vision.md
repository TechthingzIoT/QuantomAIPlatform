# President Paul Kagame and Africa's AI Vision

Source: Official Government of Rwanda publications

## Africa Must Act

President Paul Kagame has repeatedly emphasized that Africa should actively embrace emerging technologies rather than remain a passive consumer of technological change.

At the Transform Africa Summit, Kagame called on African leaders and industry to embrace artificial intelligence and make it work for Africa.

## Purpose-Driven AI

At the 2025 Transform Africa Summit, Kagame emphasized that Africa's success with AI depends not simply on adopting AI quickly, but on applying it strategically to Africa's own development priorities.

The emphasis includes context-specific applications that address African needs.

## African Ownership

Rwanda's broader AI agenda emphasizes building African capability rather than treating Africa only as a market for technologies developed elsewhere.

The 2025 Global AI Summit on Africa in Kigali highlighted African AI development, sovereign computing infrastructure, digital networks, open data and African AI talent.

## Relevance to QAIR

QAIR demonstrates a technical principle consistent with this direction:

AI inference does not inherently need to depend on a remote AI service.

A language model can be deployed locally, executed locally and combined with locally controlled knowledge.

This enables experimentation with AI infrastructure under the control of the organization or institution operating it.

## Important Qualification

QAIR should not claim that President Kagame specifically proposed QAIR, GGUF, llama.cpp or offline LLM inference.

QAIR is an independent engineering project demonstrating how local AI infrastructure can contribute to practical experimentation with AI sovereignty.
