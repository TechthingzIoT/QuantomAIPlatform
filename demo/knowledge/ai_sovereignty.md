# AI Sovereignty

## Definition

AI sovereignty refers broadly to the ability of a country, organization or institution to maintain meaningful control over important parts of its AI capabilities, infrastructure, data and deployment.

## Major Components

AI sovereignty can involve:

1. Compute
2. Data
3. Models
4. Infrastructure
5. Talent
6. Cybersecurity
7. Governance
8. Deployment capability

## Local Inference

Local inference is one component of this broader picture.

When inference occurs locally, an organization can reduce dependence on external inference infrastructure.

However, local inference alone does not constitute complete AI sovereignty.

## QAIR's Role

QAIR focuses specifically on the local inference layer.

Its purpose is to demonstrate that an organization can:

- Deploy a language model locally.
- Run inference locally.
- Maintain a local knowledge base.
- Retrieve knowledge locally.
- Generate responses locally.
- Expose the capability through a local API.

## Demonstration Question

The key question for the QAIR showcase is:

"Can useful AI inference and knowledge retrieval continue when the Internet is unavailable?"

QAIR is designed to demonstrate that capability.
