# Quantom Assist Solutions

## Vision

Quantom Assist Solutions envisions supporting the digital transformation revolution across Africa through:

- STEM education
- Talent and skills development
- Professional development
- Entrepreneurial development
- AgriTech
- Product development
- Research

The vision is to contribute to an Africa where professionals, entrepreneurs and young innovators can access the knowledge, skills and technological capabilities required to participate in the digital transformation of the continent.

## Origin

Quantom Assist Solutions started in Nigeria.

The company was pioneered by its visionaries:

- Mustafa S. Bello
- Jude Mike Baba

## Areas of Focus

### STEM Education

Quantom Assist Solutions supports STEM education as a foundation for developing future technology professionals, engineers, researchers and innovators.

### Talent and Skills Development

The organization focuses on developing practical digital and technical skills for professionals, entrepreneurs and emerging innovators.

### AgriTech

Quantom Assist Solutions explores technology-driven approaches to agriculture, including the application of IoT, AI and other emerging technologies to agricultural challenges.

### Product Development

The organization supports the development of technology products designed to address practical problems and opportunities in African markets.

### Research

Research and experimentation are important components of the organization's approach to technology development and innovation.

## Relationship to QAIR

QAIR is a technology project within the broader ecosystem of innovation represented by Quantom Assist Solutions.

QAIR demonstrates local AI inference and knowledge retrieval using locally controlled computing infrastructure.

This makes QAIR relevant to the broader objective of developing African technical capability and practical AI infrastructure.

## African Digital Transformation

Quantom Assist Solutions' vision is broader than artificial intelligence alone.

Its focus encompasses education, skills, entrepreneurship, agriculture, product development and research as interconnected components of Africa's digital transformation.

QAIR represents one technical component within this broader vision.
