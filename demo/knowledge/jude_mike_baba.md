## QAIR

Jude is the engineer and builder behind QAIR.

QAIR explores the ability to run artificial intelligence locally using locally controlled computing infrastructure.

The project focuses on:

- Local AI inference
- GGUF language models
- llama.cpp-based inference
- Local knowledge retrieval
- Retrieval-augmented generation
- AI experimentation without dependence on external AI inference APIs

QAIR is intended as a practical engineering exploration of local AI infrastructure and AI sovereignty.
